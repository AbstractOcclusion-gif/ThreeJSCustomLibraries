Craters

All modules ↗

Terrain craters wired to particles and Jolt: blast the ground, or drop a boulder or a crate and its own shape makes the crater. Shards and dust come from the particles module, the clods are Jolt bodies, both coloured by the strata the crater cut.

Drag to orbit when no click action is chosen, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
