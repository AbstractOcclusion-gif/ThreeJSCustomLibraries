Craters

All modules ↗

Terrain craters wired to particles and Jolt: blast the ground, or launch an object (rock, boulder, crate, beam, slab, plane): its weight, speed, angle and outline make the crater. Shards and dust come from the particles module, the clods are Jolt bodies, both coloured by the strata the crater cut. Grass is cleared and trees are blown apart or tipped over.

Drag to orbit when no click action is chosen, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
